# Story — Sentinel Strike

Original setting (no borrowed names, places or plots). Tone: grounded near-future, PEGI 12 —
players are "eliminated", nobody bleeds. The text players see lives in
`packages/content/src/lore.json` and each map's `location` / `description`.

## Premise

2071. Eleven years after the **Blackout Winter** took the satellites down, the only long-range
      signal left runs through **the Relay**: a chain of ground stations along the **Saltline
      coast**. Whoever holds the relays holds trade, power and the news everyone lives by.

## Factions

- **Aegis Directive** (blue) — _"Hold the line. Keep the signal."_ A security directive hired by
  the coast cities to guard the Relay. Drilled and well supplied, paid in access fees the inland
  towns can't afford. They keep the lights on — for whoever pays.
- **Ember Syndicate** (orange) — _"Burn bright. Take it back."_ Salvage crews from the inland
  scrap towns, shut out of the Relay by Directive tariffs. They strip stations for parts and jam
  the signal to force a new deal. They fight for the people left in the dark — and break what
  everyone needs.

Neither side is the "good guys": that's deliberate, so either team is fun to play.

## Season 0 — Static

Ember crews have started jamming the Relay from inside the stations; the Directive has pulled
every squad to the coast. Every match is a skirmish for one relay site.

## Places

- **Relay Yard** — Switching Yard 7, the last hub still switching traffic between the coast
  cities. Whoever holds the central platform holds the uplink.
- **Saltline Depot** — a coastal freight depot at dusk where Syndicate crews ship salvaged
  relay cores out by sea.

## Where the story can grow
